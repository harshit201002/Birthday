# Magical post illustration

Original artwork generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool. It is a decorative asset, not a handwritten letter or a reproduction of either supplied reference. The envelope crest is a separate image asset documented in hogwarts-crest.md.

Final generation prompt:

Create an original transparent-background watercolor illustration asset for an elegant romantic magical correspondence webpage. Wide horizontal composition, approximately 3:1 aspect ratio. Two separate, balanced small decorative vignettes at the far left quarter and far right quarter, with the entire middle half completely empty and transparent, and transparent space around every object. LEFT: a cute kindly elderly wizard with a soft ivory beard, dusty blue pointed hat with a few antique gold stars, muted burgundy and blue robes, holding a slender wand; beside and slightly above him a small white barn owl carrying a cream envelope sealed in burgundy wax. RIGHT: a charming stack of three vintage cream, faded blush, and warm brown leather magical suitcases with antique brass clasps, with a small rustic broomstick resting diagonally beside them. Delicate hand-painted watercolor storybook illustration, fine warm pencil outlines, soft pigment washes, beautiful tiny details, muted cream, dusty blush, burgundy, antique gold accents. Friendly and whimsical, premium rather than cartoon clip art. No lettering, no words, no logos, no crests, no recognizable existing characters, no emoji, no background scene, no ground plane, no framing, no page or envelope UI. Reference direction: cute watercolor wizard school stationery embellishments. This is illustration artwork only; keep central blank area truly transparent.
The served images use transparent WebP exports: the crest has 192px and 384px variants; the watercolor illustration has 768px, 1086px, 1440px, and 2172px responsive variants. They were resized/compressed from the original PNG assets without changing the illustration design.
